Privacy Policy for ZapQR: UPI & Text QR Maker

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ZapQR: UPI & Text QR Maker ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information.

Information We Process:

The App processes the following user-provided data solely on your device:

• Payment information: UPI ID, Payee Name, Amount, and Transaction Note.

• Text input: Any text, links, or characters you enter to create a QR code.

How Your Information Is Used

• All data entered into the App is processed locally in real time on your device to create custom UPI payment links and standard QR code images.

• The App does not perform transactions or process payments directly.

Data Storage and Sharing

• Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, store, log, or transmit your UPI details, personal names, payment amounts, or custom text inputs on any remote server, database, or cloud infrastructure.

• No Third-Party Sharing: Your inputs are never sold, rented, tracked, or shared with third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or external partners.

• Device Storage: Generated QR code images are saved to your local device gallery only when you explicitly choose to export or save them.

Permissions

The App may request local device permissions (such as storage or gallery access) strictly to save or share generated QR code images upon your request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
